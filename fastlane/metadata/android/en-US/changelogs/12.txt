There is an About screen now, in the menu on the home screen. It asks Android what permissions this install actually requests rather than repeating a claim, and the full licences for every bundled library open from it. Gander no longer takes part in Android's app backup, which was copying your file names into a Drive backup in exchange for nothing. Phones whose WebView comes from the manufacturer are recognised properly now, so PDFs no longer fail there with a confusing error.
