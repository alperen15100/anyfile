Privacy Policy for ANYFILE

Last updated: August 21, 2026

ANYFILE, provided by Ecrin Labs, is designed as an offline file utility application. We respect your privacy and aim to process files locally on your device whenever supported by the application.

Information Collection

ANYFILE does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through the application.

Files and Documents

Files selected or opened by the user are processed locally on the user's device. ANYFILE does not intentionally upload users' files or documents to Ecrin Labs servers.

Internet Access

The application is designed to operate without requiring an Internet connection for its core file-processing features.

Data Sharing

Ecrin Labs does not sell users' personal information or files. The application does not intentionally share users' files with Ecrin Labs.

Third-Party Services

ANYFILE may include open-source software libraries required for application functionality. Use of open-source libraries does not by itself mean that personal files are transmitted to their developers.

Children's Privacy

ANYFILE is a general-purpose file utility and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Security

Because supported file operations are performed locally on the user's device, users retain control over the files they choose to open or process with ANYFILE.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
