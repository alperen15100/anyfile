# Willowmere Phase 3 — site notes

Running notes from the depot. Anything here that matters ends up in the
formal report; this file is just the working record.

## 29 July, morning walkover

Ground still hard in compartments 1 and 3, so the quadrat pegs went in with
difficulty and two of them had to be moved by about a metre. Recorded the
offsets on the field sheet.

- Reed front has pushed roughly 4 m further east than the March mark
- Otter spraint on the north bank, second sighting this month
- Water clear at the outlet, cloudy at the northern culvert as expected

> The settlement pocket is the thing to watch. It was designed for a 49 NTU
> load and it is taking more than twice that. If we let it fill over the
> winter we will be dredging the lagoon instead, which costs ten times as much.
